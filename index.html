The Four Pillars

What a strong mind is built on

Discipline

Do what needs to be done, even when it's hard. Champions train on the days they don't feel like it, because habits beat mood.

Tip: Lay out your kit the night before.

Motivation

Keep your why in mind. Write down the reason you play and read it before every session, especially after a bad match.

Tip: Set one small weekly goal and tick it off.

Sleep

Better sleep equals better performance. Muscles repair and skills settle in while you rest, so aim for 8-10 hours.

Tip: No screens for the last hour before bed.

Handling Rejection

Not every trial will work. Scouts pass on good players all the time, so treat a no as feedback, not a verdict.

Tip: Ask what to improve, then train exactly that.

Daily Habits

Ten minutes a day is enough. Do these in the same order every day so they become automatic.

  • Visualise your best moment on the pitch (2 min)
  • Write down one goal for today (2 min)
  • Stretch and breathe slowly (4 min)
  • Review your last match or session (2 min)

After Every Match

Ask yourself three questions

1. What went well?

2. What will I fix this week?

3. What did I learn?

Players who reflect improve faster, and it keeps one bad game from becoming a bad month.
