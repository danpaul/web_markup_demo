Last Saturday at the Peel Palace

On 29 August 2026, the house banana returned to its native habitat: the dance floor. The BPM hit 118, the lights went potassium-gold, and everyone felt the peel.

Regulars still talk about dancer نانا Peel sharing the floor with Mirror Mode Nana. In Japanese the fruit is バナナ(banana). One chalkboard still mispells the club as “Peel Palance.”

A banana that will not dance is only a snack waiting to happen.

— from The Potassium Papers: A History of Peel Palace

The peel-out is the signature step: a sharp spin that must land on the beat. The a tempo rule is simple. As the MC put it, If you bruise, you lose. Do not bring real fruit onto the floor.

The yellow in the air is no accident. Ripe banana aroma comes from isoamyl acetate, C7H14O2, which the fog machine claims to honor in spirit only. Last week’s half-price peel punch is gone; tickets are full price again.

Featured tonight: the house banana, live, unpeeled, unstoppable.
