Mushroom Kingdom Grid

Princess Peach

The ruler of the Mushroom Kingdom is often kidnapped, yet she can hold her own in a fight.

Bowser

The fire-breathing king of the Koopas keeps trying to take over Peach's castle.

Yoshi

This friendly dinosaur carries Mario on his back and swallows enemies with a long tongue.

Toad

A loyal mushroom retainer guards Peach's castle and cheers the heroes on from the sidelines.

Princess Daisy

The energetic princess of Sarasaland prefers sports and adventure to sitting in a castle.

Wario
