Technical Training

MASTER THE BALL

Great players are made with thousands of quiet touches. Train your technique, learn your role and repeat it until it is automatic.

See drills

Core Skills

Four Drills to Do Every Week

1

Passing

Wall passes with both feet. Aim at a spot, pass hard and keep it on the floor.

Do: 2 × 50 passes, each foot.

2

Dribbling

Weave through 6 cones using small touches. Keep your head up between touches.

Do: 8 runs, change foot each run.

3

First Touch

Throw the ball up or against a wall and control it dead in one touch.

Do: 3 sets of 20 touches.

4

Finishing

Shoot from the edge of the box, low and into the corners. Weak foot counts double.

Do: 10 shots each foot.

Know Your Role

Position Tips

Goalkeeper

  • Work on footwork and positioning
  • Practice distribution with both feet
  • Communicate loudly

Defender

  • Stay low, stay on your toes
  • Win your first duel with timing, not force
  • Scan before the ball arrives

Midfielder

  • Check your shoulder constantly
  • Play one- and two-touch
  • Build stamina for both boxes

Striker

  • Move before the pass is played
  • Finish with both feet
  • Stay calm when you miss

Best results come from doing both: team sessions for the game, solo sessions for your technique.

Where to Train

With a Team vs. Alone

Alone

  • Thousands of extra touches
  • Fix weak foot and weak skills
  • Train on your own schedule
  • Builds discipline and self-belief

With a Team

  • Decision-making under pressure
  • Game speed and communication
  • Learning positions and patterns
  • Feedback from a coach
